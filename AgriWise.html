How it works

One photo in, a fair sale out.

Five steps connect the field to the buyer — no expert visit and no middleman required.

1

Capture

Farmer photographs a crop leaf, online or off.

2

Diagnose

The CNN model detects disease and severity in seconds.

3

Treat

Farmer follows an organic or chemical treatment plan.

4

List

Healthy produce goes up for sale with fair pricing.

5

Sell

Buyer orders directly — no middleman in between.

Two engines, one platform

Everything a farmer needs, in one app.

01

Instant Photo Diagnosis

Upload or capture a leaf image; get a disease name and confidence score in seconds.

02

Severity & Treatment

Organic and chemical treatment options, ranked by severity: low, medium, high.

03

Diagnosis History

Every farmer keeps a timeline of past scans, crops, and outcomes.

04

Expert Escalation

Low-confidence cases route automatically to a verified agri-expert.

05

Offline Capture

Photos are captured in the field with no signal, and sync once connectivity returns.

06

Disease Encyclopedia

A searchable library of crop → common disease → symptoms → prevention.

01

List & Discover Produce

Farmers list crops with photos, quantity, price and harvest date; buyers filter by location.

02

Buy Farm Inputs

Verified sellers offer seeds, fertiliser, pesticides and equipment rental.

03

Chat & Negotiate

In-app chat or click-to-call connects buyers and farmers directly.

04

Orders & Payments

Cart, checkout, cash-on-delivery or online payment, with live order tracking.

05

Ratings & Trust Score

Two-way reviews build a reliable, accountable trading community.

06

Live Mandi Prices

Nearby market price reference, so farmers always price fairly.
